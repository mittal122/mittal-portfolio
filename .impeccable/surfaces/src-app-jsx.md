## Direction contract

THESIS: A full-bleed shader portal where the field is the infrastructure Mittal works on: a living interference current that the projects sit on. It refuses the dark-purple glow kit of stacked react-bits effects and same-size cards; one shader, one display face, everything else quiet.

OWN-WORLD: Void-indigo ground (#090B18 to #1B1F3D) driven by a single fragment shader of flowing interference bands in lilac fading into one ember current, with fine film grain. Mist type. Ember is the only incandescent accent: active and live states, the secondary action; the primary action is a solid lilac fill. Tall condensed display (Antonio) at huge size against tiny tracked caps (Schibsted Grotesk); Martian Mono only for data: years, stack, status. (Geist was replaced after the detector flagged it as overused.) Thin 1px hairlines, a crosshair tick on the top rule, outlined controls, a dotted menu button.

STORY: The visitor reads who Mittal is and his stage in one glance, sees that the projects make AI advise while deterministic code acts, opens one, and writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top hairline bar with the wordmark at left and GitHub, email and menu at right. A left rail of route links with a dot on the current route. MITTAL / DOMADIYA in two condensed lines at about 11rem, with the role line in tracked caps and the one-sentence thesis below. Primary SEE THE PROJECTS (lilac fill) and secondary WRITE TO ME (ember outline). Right edge: a vertical scroll rail; a bottom-right status readout reading OPEN TO FULL-TIME ROLES · 2027. The bottom band starts the selected projects row: Helmsman, AutoSRE and CloudScope, each with its own line-drawn mechanism diagram.

FORM: WebGL shader portal (dealt challenger "webgl-shader-portal", adopted by the user over the assigned Engineering Notebook). Seed key c0a1eef9. Signature interaction: the cursor bends the field locally, and the native cursor stays visible. Route changes run a short shader dissolve, with no fake loader. Motion grammar: the field drifts slowly; content enters once with an exponential ease-out from visible. Reduced motion freezes the field to a single still frame and removes the dissolve. Deliberate deviations: no custom cursor, no scroll inertia, no percentage loader, and the display face exceeds 6rem because the world demands it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
